Welcome to my webpage.

I’m an independent researcher focused on Balkan histories and narratives in relation to broader European and transnational contexts. My work draws on a range of sources, from archival documents to audio-visual material. My main interests revolve around the intersections between the state, nationalism, racism, and socialism. Besides publishing essays in academic journals and magazines, I organise film screenings.

On this site, you’ll find links to my work, information about my current projects, and reflections on how to cope with the increasing far-right trends.
